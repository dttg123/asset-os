# Integrated consistency — v0.7.13

Display identity is shared by overview recent transactions, the ledger/search, transaction detail, monthly cash-flow grouping, spending, card purchase rows, bill details and linked card schedules. Expense/refund categories retain user overrides; imported statement rows without a confirmed category remain unclassified, explicit Other is preserved, and reliable insurance schedule metadata supplies Insurance. Purchase/card/schedule records and amounts are not rewritten for display corrections.

The overview stays monthly and has no analysis chart or period controls. Spending item details lead with their selected-period total and recent records; the secondary trend is collapsed behind a disclosure. Missing comparison-history panels are omitted. Card rows include their category and use the classified purchase name. Transaction detail exposes the same classification action and links directly to its related card purchase/payment detail. Schedule groups use Income, Card, Insurance, Loan, Saving/Investment and Living Expense consistently.

Loan principal and interest have separate operation labels. Monthly operating-cash change is labelled Cash Change throughout; an older wrapper incorrectly replaced it with Available Cash. Card interest refunds are identified separately and do not become purchase consumption.

PWA update listeners are installed before register/update can finish. Duplicate controller events are ignored; first installation does not reload. Existing sessions check for updates when they return to the foreground. Replacement controllers reload once after open sheets/dialogs and local saves finish, preserving active input. The obsolete per-build session reload marker was removed from release synchronization. The user's photographed stale screen cannot be attributed to one device-specific cause from screenshots alone; the missed-listener race is reproduced separately and covered by a regression.

New browser checks follow imported card purchases across overview, ledger, transaction detail, spending, card hub, schedule, actual payment and refund; verify explicit Other and renamed items survive reload without source rewrites; cover income/saving/insurance/loan labels and cash-change wording; and perform a real service-worker replacement while preserving an open draft. Existing finance, source preservation, pagination, view-only storage isolation, narrow layouts, ZIP restore and lifecycle checks remain enabled.
